When you think of breakfast, what is the first meal you think of? Is it cereal, pancakes, oatmeal or eggs, meat, and bread? In our American culture, we have been programed to eat a breakfast high in sugar or from a fast-food restaurant.  This kind of meal doesn't satisfy the body and develops an addiction to things high in sugar and refined oils.

As I have learned more about food over the years, I realize how important it is to start your day off with a nutritious meal to set the tone for the rest of the day.
