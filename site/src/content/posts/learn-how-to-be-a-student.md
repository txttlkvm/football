Have you ever desired to learn a new skill or hobby? Then you realize you're a wife, mom of five children and many more roles Yes, that was me! I thought of all the reasons why I shouldn't start something new instead of thinking of the many reasons why I should.

Recently, I began piano lessons with the intention that one day I would be able to play piano with my children and encourage them to go further in their learning. Three out of five of our children have piano lessons. I am amazed at how much they have learned in a short amount of time. Although learning to play the piano at age 32 can seem a little intimidating I was eager to take on the challenge.

As a child, I don't remember having the desire to play the piano. It's always been an instrument that I love to hear other people play but the thought of playing myself was inconceivable. The sound of hearing my children play the piano as I tried my best to help them practice encouraged me to see that I could either be an observer or a participator in what they are learning. I choose to participate because music is one of the many ways we can bring honor to God. Instead of saying, “My children know how to play the piano”, what if I could say, “we play the piano together.”

&#x20;As they have seen me walk out of the door to go to my lesson or sit on the side to watch me play, I often think what is this doing for my children? Could it be that their eyes are delighting in seeing their mom join them in the hard work it takes to learn piano?

&#x20;As a homeschooling mom, I urge my children to learn how to be a student. Not only because they are my students but also because in life, we are constantly learning.
