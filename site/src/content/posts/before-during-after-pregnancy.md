There is a lot to prepare for when you are having a baby. My hope is that I can help moms who may not know what to get for themselves or their baby as they prepare. Below I have created a running list of things that have helped me before, during and after pregnancy. Since I have had four babies back-to-back, I am still using some of these items. I hope this list relieves pressure from the to do list you already have.
