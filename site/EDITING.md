# How to edit the website (for Diamond)

1. Go to **https://www.marriagemotherhood-meals.org/edit/** (bookmark it!) and tap **Open my editor**. Sign in with your email and password. You will land on a friendly start screen — tap **Write a new post** or anything else.
2. Pick what you want to change:
   - **Blog Posts** → click a post to edit it, or **Create New** to write one. Add a cover photo, tick categories, write in the big box (use the toolbar for bold, links, photos), and press **Save**.
   - **Pages** → About Me, Kids Classes, Bakery. Same idea. Turn on "Show the Register box" for any page that needs the sign-up button.
   - **Site Settings** → menu, colors and fonts, welcome banner, announcement bar, class card, footer.
3. **Posts save by themselves** while they are drafts. Other pages (Home, About, Settings) wait until you press **Save**, so nothing half-written goes live. **Draft:** tick "Draft" to keep a post hidden. **Schedule:** set "Publish on a future date" and it stays hidden until then.
4. To add a **video, a photo with a caption, photos side by side, or a button**, click **Embed** in the toolbar above the writing box. For videos, paste a YouTube or Vimeo link (best for long videos), or upload a short MP4.
4. **Home Page** is built from sections (big banner, highlight card, posts grid, photo gallery, quote, questions, columns, video and more). Click **+** to add a section, drag to reorder, click one to edit it.
5. The **live preview** (on the right on a computer, above the form on a phone) shows the page as it will look on a phone or a computer, and updates as you type. **Full screen** pops it into its own window.
6. After **Save**, the site updates by itself in about a minute.

Made a mistake? Everything is saved in Git history, so any version can be restored. Ask Claude.

## New things you can do
- **Start from a template:** on the start screen tap **Start from a template** for a ready-made page (landing, class, about, menu, contact) or post (recipe, story, weekly update). Tap **Change my look** to try a whole new color and font style in one tap.
- **Undo / redo:** the **↶ Undo** and **↷ Redo** buttons above the preview (or Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z). **🕘 Earlier versions** brings back an older saved version from this device.
- **Tags:** on a post, add tags like “sourdough”. Readers can click a tag to see similar posts (`/tag/sourdough/`).
- **Comments:** turn on in **Logo, menu & more → Reader comments** (free Disqus account needed).
- **Scheduled posts:** set “Schedule” on a post. A background job checks every few hours and publishes it once the date passes.
- **Section look:** every section has “Section look” — change spacing, width, a background photo, or a link name.
- **Side by side:** the “Side by side” section puts any two things next to each other (photo + words, quote + button…).
