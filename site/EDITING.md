# How to edit the website (for Diamond)

1. Go to **https://www.marriagemotherhood-meals.org/admin/** and log in with your email and password.
2. Pick what you want to change:
   - **Blog Posts** → click a post to edit it, or **Create New** to write one. Add a cover photo, tick categories, write in the big box (use the toolbar for bold, links, photos), and press **Save**.
   - **Pages** → About Me, Kids Classes, Bakery. Same idea. Turn on "Show the Register box" for any page that needs the sign-up button.
   - **Site Settings** → menu, colors and fonts, welcome banner, announcement bar, class card, footer.
3. **Draft:** tick "Draft" to keep a post hidden. **Schedule:** set "Publish on a future date" and it stays hidden until then.
3. To add a **video, a photo with a caption, photos side by side, or a button**, click **Embed** in the toolbar above the writing box. For videos, paste a YouTube or Vimeo link (best for long videos), or upload a short MP4.
4. **Home Page** is built from sections (big banner, highlight card, posts grid, photo gallery, quote, questions, columns, video and more). Click **+** to add a section, drag to reorder, click one to edit it.
5. The **Live preview** at the top of every form shows the page as it will look on a phone or a computer, and updates as you type. **Open big** pops it into its own window.
6. After **Save**, the site updates by itself in about a minute.

Made a mistake? Everything is saved in Git history, so any version can be restored. Ask Claude.
